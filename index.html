Mission 90% Blueprint: Phase 1 to Phase 3 Roadmap

Actionable strategy for you and your study partner to score 90%+ aggregate.

The 90% Formula

80 Marks Theory Paper + 20 Marks School INA (Internal Assessment). Agar School se 19-20 Marks pakke hain, toh Board Paper mein sirf 70/80 laana hai! Bilkul aasan aur achievable target hai.

Phase 1 45 Days

Core Syllabus Sprint (Nov 1 - Dec 15)

High-weightage aur foundation chapters ko 100% complete karna hai. Roz 5 ghante pure concentration ke sath lagao. Har chapter ke baad textbook back exercises zaroor karo.

Phase 2 30 Days

PYQs & Model Papers (Dec 16 - Jan 15)

Board ke pichhle 5 saal ke question papers aur official Model Test Papers solve karo. Bar-bar repeat hone wale questions ko note karo aur unhe master karo.

Phase 3 25 Days

Full Mock Tests & Writing (Jan 16 - Boards)

Roz ek full paper 3 ghante ka timer laga kar likho. Speed, handwriting aur presentation improve karo. Mistakes ko analyse karke revise karo.

Mathematics (Target: 75+/80)

  • High Weightage: Real Numbers, Polynomials, Linear & Quadratic Equations, Coordinate Geometry, Triangles, Stats & Probability.
  • Theorems: Thales Theorem (BPT) aur Circle theorems ko 5-5 baar likh kar pakka karo (fixed 6 marks).
  • Golden Rule: Maths padhi nahi jaati, rough copy par ghis kar solve ki jaati hai!

Science (Target: 72+/80)

  • Diagrams: Human Eye, Ray Diagrams (Mirrors & Lenses), Digestive System, Heart/Kidney Diagrams.
  • Reactions: Chemical equations balance karna aur reactivity series yaad rakhna mandatory hai.
  • Numericals: Light (Mirror/Lens formula) aur Electricity (Ohm's Law, Series-Parallel) roz 2 solve karo.

Social Studies (SST) (Target: 75+/80)

  • Map Work: 100% full marks milte hain! Rivers, Dams, Mineral belts, Punjab borders roz 5 min practice karo.
  • Headings Trick: Long answers ko paragraph me mat likho. Dark headings aur 4-5 bullet points bana kar likho.
  • Civics & History: Direct dates aur events ki ek one-page timeline bana lo.

Languages (Punjabi, English, Hindi)

  • Game Changer: Ye teeno subjects tumhara total percentage 90% tak kheenchengi!
  • Grammar: Muhavare, Ling/Vachan, Tense me zero marks kat te hain agar sahi ho.
  • Writing Formats: Letter, Application, Paragraph writing ka exact board format follow karo.
